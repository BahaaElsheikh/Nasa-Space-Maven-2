How are NASA references used?

Some component forms and functions are inspired by systems documented on NASA rovers, including six-wheel mobility, cameras, the robotic arm, sample-handling tools, and power systems. This comparison is educational and approximate; it is not an official NASA endorsement. Any component marked as a “game concept” is not claimed to be installed on a specific rover.

Mass and power values are illustrative game-balancing estimates, not manufacturing data or NASA specifications. This 3D model is conceptual; it is not CAD or an engineering simulation.
