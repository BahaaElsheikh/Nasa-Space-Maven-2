# Stage 2: Mission Preparation & Transit

Stage 2 challenges the player with cleanroom equipment payload selection and the interplanetary Earth-Mars transit simulation.

## Subdirectories

- `cutscene/`: Narrative transition slides (`1.png`, `2.png`, `3.png`), audio narration, and `story.json`.
- `sprites/`: Cleanroom equipment and transit payload sprites (`commander.png`, `oxygen_tank.png`, `water_recycler.png`, `food_supply.png`, `medical_kit.png`, `communication.png`, `power_unit.png`, `habitat_module.png`, `scientific_tools.png`, `rover.png`).
